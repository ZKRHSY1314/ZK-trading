## Semantics stated explicitly for Codex

* A gap at execution **downsizes the intent itself** (the unfilled remainder of the reduced quantity is released when the reduced intent completes); a valid contemporaneous print that affords nothing cancels the intent. Both are declared contract choices, not fabricated fills.
* The current-risk re-check values the target symbol at the observed execution print and other holdings at the latest causally eligible mark; without such a mark the buy is refused rather than valued at zero or cost.
* Kernel-rejected attempts (other than pure affordability in the dry run) leave the intent and its reservation untouched; the ledger's own atomicity is unchanged.
