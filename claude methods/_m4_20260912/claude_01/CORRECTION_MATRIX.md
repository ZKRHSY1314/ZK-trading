## Items left for Codex

None of the six findings is disputed. Two consequences of the fixes are stated explicitly so Codex can check them rather than infer them:

* Partial **buy** fills remain whole-lot (`buy_increment`, ≥ `min_buy_quantity`). A venue may fill a buy order in odd quantities; the contract deliberately does not invent such fills without a declared policy. If M4-02/M4-03 needs share-granular buy partials, it must be a declared `LotPolicy` extension, not a silent change.
* For an `open_auction` / `close_auction` order the "live" flag refers to the next auction of that phase inside the window; a caller that wants to convert an unfilled auction order into a continuous order must submit a new order (new `order_id`, `eligible_from` after the auction) — the kernel never re-phases an order.
